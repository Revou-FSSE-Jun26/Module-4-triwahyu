# Product Context — RevoTask
- RevoTask is a Trello-like task-management application.
- Board members create/manage boards, organize columns, move cards by drag-and-drop, comment, and track assigned work.
- Board administrators manage member access, oversee cards, and monitor activity through analytics and notifications.
- Prioritize clear task ownership, predictable board interactions, and reliable persistence.
- Do not invent product behavior absent from the active feature specification.
